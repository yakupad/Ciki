Çıkı is your household's shared budget book. It's named after the knotted cloth people once tied their savings in. Keep your cards, loans, rent, bills and salaries in one place, and see past months, this month and upcoming months at a glance.

SEE YOUR MONTH-END TODAY
• Month-end net, income and expenses on one card
• A per-person view of who pays and earns what
• A 13-month chart; upcoming months are estimated from recurring payments
• Payments due this month, sorted by due day

KEEP YOUR SPREADSHEET HABITS
• Table view: months across, items down
• Paid, pending and skipped-this-month states
• Installments, copy from last month, amount suggestions

USE IT TOGETHER
• Share your household over iCloud with your partner, family or housemates
• Everyone sees and edits the same entries, and you can see who changed what
• Unlimited people with relationship labels

CURRENCY AND IBAN
• 21 currencies at central bank rates; view totals in the currency you choose
• Paid foreign-currency entries keep the rate from the payment day
• An IBAN book for your landlord, building fees and more, copied with one tap

REMINDERS AND SHORTCUTS
• Notifications before each due day
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
• Apple Watch app and watch face complications
• CSV export as an entry list or a spreadsheet-style table

ON EVERY DEVICE
• iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch
• A two-pane layout that follows the fold on iPhone Duo
• Light and dark mode, English and Turkish

YOUR PRIVACY STAYS YOURS
• No data collected: no ads, analytics or tracking
• Your entries stay on your device and in your own iCloud account
• Face ID lock, hidden amounts in notifications, delete all your data
